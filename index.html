Road Trip Showdown

The license plate game. Look out the window; the phone keeps score.

How to play

Pick your home state, add the players, and set what the winner gets. When someone spots a plate, a passenger taps that player, then taps the state on the map. The announcer reads the score aloud so nobody has to look at the screen. The driver just drives.

The first time a place is called on a drive, it's worth double. Random power-ups triple everything for a few minutes. Whoever spots the most different places gets their total multiplied by 1.5. Temporary plates, military, tribal plates and Teslas never count.

Common questions

I can't hear the announcer. Check the volume, and that "Grandma reads the scores" is on. On iPhone, the announcer still speaks with the ring switch on silent.

I want a different voice. Tap Announcer on the home screen and try the voices on your device. You can add more under Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices.

I scored the wrong thing. Tap Undo in the bar that appears for a few seconds after every score.

How do I start my collection over? Tap States collected on the home screen, then hold the reset button at the top.
