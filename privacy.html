Privacy Policy

Road Trip Showdown · Effective September 2026

The short version

Road Trip Showdown does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything the game remembers stays on your device.

What stays on your device

So the game can pick up where you left off, the app saves the following on your phone, tablet or browser only:

  • The names you type for players and teams, your home state, and the rewards you set.
  • The current drive's scores and your collection of states.
  • Your settings: the announcer's voice and which announcer lines have already been used.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. We have no server and cannot see it. Deleting the app removes all of it.

Speech

The announcer uses your device's built-in text-to-speech to read scores aloud. The app does not record audio or use the microphone.

Location

The app does not request or use your location.

Children

The game can be played by families with children. Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, for example by adding online group play, this page will be updated before that version is released.
